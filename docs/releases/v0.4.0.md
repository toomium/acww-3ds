## What's new

- **The Switch.** The port now runs on a Nintendo Switch with custom firmware (Atmosphère), from the Homebrew Menu:
  `acww.nro`, beside `game.pak` in `sdmc:/switch/acww/`. The web builder asks for your console and makes the ZIP for
  it. On the Switch:
  - **60 frames a second**, docked or handheld, in 720p by default (`ACWW_SWITCH_SCALE`, `1` to `4`, after a restart).
  - **16:9 and the wide view.** The town is drawn across the whole display, the game's interface at its edges.
  - **One screen.** The world fills the display; the touch screen appears as a panel when it has something to show.
    Six other layouts (side by side, stacked, ...): press the left stick (L3) for the next one, it is kept in
    `acww.cfg` and can be set on the options page too. The right stick (R3) shows or hides the touch screen.
  - **Touch when docked.** The right stick moves a pointer, ZR touches with it. Handheld, the touch screen touches
    where the game's touch screen is shown.
  - **The analog walk.** The left stick walks at any angle and speed, as the 3DS's Circle Pad does.
- **A new name.** The project is now **acww-port** (github.com/toomium/acww-port), for both consoles. The old links
  lead there.

Nothing changes on the 3DS: the program behaves as in v0.3.0.

## Updating

**3DS:** replace `acww.3dsx` on the SD card (`sdmc:/3ds/acww/`). `game.pak`, `acww.cfg` and your save stay as they
are; the HOME Menu version (`acww.cia`) doesn't need to be installed again.

**Switch (new):** in the web builder, pick the Switch and build the ZIP from your ROM, then copy its `switch` folder to
the root of the SD card. Or put `acww.nro` and a `game.pak` in `sdmc:/switch/acww/` by hand: the 3DS's `game.pak` works there too.

## Known issues

- On the Switch, a door frame can flicker a little.
- The Switch port is new: please report anything odd with the `log.txt` beside the program.
- On an Old 3DS the game still runs slower than on the DS (about 34 of 60 frames a second in town); making it faster
  is being worked on.
- The HOME Menu version's banner is a placeholder.
- Only the USA ROM (Rev 1) is supported. Other languages still work by hand (`ACWW_LANG` in `acww.cfg`, with the
  European ROM as `lang.nds`); the web builder doesn't set them up.
- Wireless play does not work.
