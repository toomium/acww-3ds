Animal Crossing: Wild World 3DS

Make the SD card's files from your own cartridge dump. Everything happens in your browser: your ROM is never uploaded.

1. Your ROM

Your own dump of Animal Crossing: Wild World (USA, Rev 1), as a .nds file.

2. The program

Looking for this site's build…

This site carries no build. Build acww.3dsx yourself (the repository's README says how) and choose it here:

3. Build

Unpack the ZIP onto the root of the SD card: it holds the folder 3ds/acww/ with acww.3dsx and game.pak (your ROM's files, without the game's code), and cias/acww.cia if you chose the HOME Menu version. The settings are on the options page in the game's pause menu. Start the game from the Homebrew Launcher. Sound needs the 3DS's DSP firmware at sd:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc, dumped from your own console.

Don't share the ZIP: it holds your cartridge's data and the game's code.
